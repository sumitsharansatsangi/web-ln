01 Introduction

Kumpali Private Limited ("Loanx", "we", "us", "our") provides the Loanx mobile application (the "App") for Android and iOS. Loanx helps individuals and small businesses maintain records of money lent against pledged assets, including borrower details, repayments, loan history, related documents, and receipts. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles, why, and what choices you have.

By downloading or using Loanx, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

02 Who this policy covers

This policy applies to anyone who downloads or uses the Loanx App from the Google Play Store or Apple App Store. It covers the App itself and any features within it, including receipt sharing through WhatsApp, Telegram, or print, and backups to Google Drive.

It does not cover third-party services you choose to share receipts through (such as WhatsApp or Telegram) or Google Drive itself — those services have their own privacy policies, linked in Section 7.

03 Information we collect

3.1 Information you enter into the App ("Ledger Data")

Loanx does not require an account or login. To use the App, you enter your own lending records directly, which may include:

  • Borrower name and, if you choose to add it, a phone number or note
  • Amount or item lent, and the date it was lent
  • Repayment status, repayment date, and any notes you add
  • Receipts you generate for sharing or printing

This Ledger Data is stored locally on your device and, if you enable backup, in your own Google Drive account. Loanx does not transmit this data to our own servers, because Loanx does not operate a server that stores your ledger.

3.2 Information collected automatically

Like most apps, Loanx may automatically collect limited technical information to keep the App working correctly, such as:

  • Device type, operating system version, and app version
  • Crash logs and basic performance diagnostics
  • Aggregated, non-identifying usage statistics (for example, which features are opened)

This diagnostic information does not include your Ledger Data and is used only to fix bugs and improve app stability.

3.3 Information from third-party services you connect

If you choose to back up or restore using Google Drive, Loanx requests limited permission through Google's sign-in and Drive APIs to create, update, or restore from its own backup file in your Drive. Loanx only accesses the specific Loanx backup file it creates — not your other Drive files, contacts, or Gmail. See Section 7 for details.

05 How we use information

  • To let you record, search, and manage your own lending records within the App
  • To generate and format receipts for you to send or print
  • To back up your Ledger Data to, or restore it from, your own Google Drive, at your request
  • To diagnose crashes and improve the App's stability and performance
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as responding to a valid legal request

We do not use your Ledger Data for advertising, and we do not sell it. Loanx does not display third-party ads.

06 Where your data lives

Your Ledger Data is stored primarily on your device. If you turn on backup, a copy is written to a file in your own Google Drive account, once per day. Loanx does not keep a separate copy of this file on any server we operate. This means:

  • If you uninstall the App without a Drive backup enabled, your local Ledger Data is deleted with it.
  • If you lose access to your Google account, we cannot recover your backup for you — it is stored under your account, governed by Google's own account-recovery process.

08 Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your information. We do not share your Ledger Data with advertisers or data brokers. We may disclose limited information only:

  • When you direct us to, by using a share or print feature
  • To service providers who help operate the App (for example, crash-reporting tools), under confidentiality obligations
  • If required by law, regulation, legal process, or a valid governmental request
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of Loanx, our users, or the public
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, with notice to you where required by law

09 Retention and deletion

Your Ledger Data is retained on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Backup files in your Google Drive are retained until you delete them from your Drive — Loanx does not automatically remove old backups. Diagnostic and crash data is retained only as long as needed to investigate and resolve issues, typically no longer than 3 months.

To delete your data: delete individual entries in the App, clear the App's local storage from your device settings, and/or delete the backup file from your Google Drive.

10 Children's privacy

Loanx is intended for adults managing personal or small-business lending records and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). If you believe a child has provided information through the App, contact us using the details in Section 15 and we will remove it.

11 Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California). Because your Ledger Data lives on your device and your own Drive, you can exercise most of these rights directly:

  • Access and export: view or export your ledger from within the App, or open your backup file directly in Google Drive.
  • Correction: edit any entry directly in the App.
  • Deletion: delete entries in the App, uninstall the App, and/or delete your Drive backup file.
  • Withdraw consent: turn off Drive backup at any time in the App's settings.

For anything we hold ourselves (such as diagnostic data), contact us using the details in Section 15 and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

12 International data transfers

Because Loanx relies on Google, Apple, WhatsApp, and Telegram infrastructure, your data may be processed on servers located outside your country, in accordance with those providers' own safeguards and policies. Loanx itself does not operate servers that store your Ledger Data.

13 Security

We rely on your device's built-in security (such as screen lock) and Google's account security for your Drive backup. We recommend enabling a passcode or biometric lock on your device, since anyone with access to your unlocked device can access the App. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example to reflect new features or legal requirements. We will update the "Last updated" date above, and for material changes, we will provide notice within the App or through the app store listing before the change takes effect.
