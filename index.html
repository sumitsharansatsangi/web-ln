The notebook problem

You already keep records. The notebook just isn't keeping up.

Pages go missing. Ink fades. And when a borrower says "I already paid you," a torn corner isn't much of an argument.

The paper ledger

Easy to lose, hard to prove

  • Entries scattered across pages or notebooks
  • No way to search when a borrower shows up
  • Receipts written by hand, easy to dispute
  • One spilled cup of tea, and it's gone
Loanx

The same habit, kept safe

  • Every loan recorded the moment you lend it
  • Find any borrower's record in seconds
  • Receipts sent instantly, with a clear timestamp
  • Backed up daily, on its own
pg. 03
What it does

Everything your ledger needs, nothing it doesn't

Loanx sticks to the job of a lender's notebook — recording, finding, and proving — and does it without asking anything extra of you.

No.FeatureWhat it means for you
001 Record as you lend Note who you're lending to, what you're lending — cash or an item — and the date. It takes about as long as writing it on paper.
002 Find them instantly Search by name when a borrower comes to repay. No flipping back through pages to find where you wrote their entry.
003 Send a receipt WhatsApp, Telegram, or a printed slip — hand your borrower proof of repayment in the format they trust.
004 Back up automatically Once a day, your ledger saves to a Google Drive account you control. Change your phone, and your records come with you.
005 No login required Open the app and start writing. There's no account to create, and no Loanx server holding onto your ledger.

pg. 05
Whose data is it

Your ledger stays yours

Loanx isn't a bank, and it doesn't want to be your record-keeper. It just helps you keep the one you already have.

  • 01

    No account, no password

    There's nothing to sign up for, so there's no Loanx account for anyone to break into.

  • 02

    Backed up to your Drive, not ours

    Daily backups go straight to the Google Drive account you're already signed into. Loanx doesn't keep a copy.

  • 03

    Receipts you can point to

    Every receipt carries the date and amount you recorded, so a repayment is never just someone's word.
