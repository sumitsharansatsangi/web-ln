Good records make good business.

Your ledger holds the history of relationships that matter. Loanx makes that history easier to record, find and stand behind—without changing the way you work.

A note on what Loanx is: it is a record-management tool. Loanx does not lend money, process payments or make lending decisions.

A ledger should feel personal

Your records are not our product.

Loanx is designed to help you manage your records—not to take ownership of them. The choices around your data stay visible and in your hands.

  • 01
    No Loanx account required

    Start with your ledger, without creating another online identity to manage.

  • 02
    Google Drive only when you choose

    Drive access is used for Loanx backup or restore when you ask for it—not for unrelated files.

  • 03
    A clear line around the product

    Loanx stores records. It does not handle money, issue credit or process payments.
